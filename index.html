Past the Label

Food science, explained calmly and honestly.

Past the Label is an English-language YouTube channel that looks behind food labels and everyday eating habits, and explains what the research actually says.

What we make

Short and long educational videos about nutrition, food processing and how the body responds to what we eat. Our videos are for education only and are not medical advice.
