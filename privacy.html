Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how Past the Label ("we", "us") handles information in connection with this website and with Past the Label Uploader, a private tool we use to manage our own YouTube channel.

1. About Past the Label Uploader

Past the Label Uploader is an internal automation tool. It is used only by the Past the Label team, and only to manage the Past the Label YouTube channel. It is not offered to, or intended for, anyone else.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with our channel you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Google user data we access

When the channel owner authorizes the tool with their Google account, it may access the following through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API:

  • Permission to upload videos to the channel and to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publishing status.
  • Permission to post and manage comments on the channel's own videos.
  • Aggregated, non-personal channel statistics, such as views, watch time, audience retention and click-through rate.

3. How we use this data

We use this access only to publish and manage videos on our own channel, and to review aggregated performance so we can plan better content. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to build profiles of viewers.

4. Storage and security

The authorization token is stored as an encrypted secret in a private code repository and is used only by our automated publishing jobs. Aggregated analytics summaries may be kept in the same private repository. We do not store passwords.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with Google, as needed to carry out the actions described above.

Past the Label Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Viewers and visitors

The tool does not collect personal information about viewers of our videos. This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

The authorizing account can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and we delete it from our repository.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to us through the About page of the Past the Label YouTube channel.
